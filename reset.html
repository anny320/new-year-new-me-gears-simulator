I created this exercise for myself. As I began thinking about my goals for 2026, I realised I felt busy and capable, yet unclear about how I was moving. I kept trying to set goals without first checking whether my energy and capacity actually supported them.

⚙️ The Gears — A Brief Introduction

The gears describe how you are operating in this season — your pace, capacity, and focus. Not ambition, performance, or potential. Alignment with your current reality.

What the gears are

  • A situational framework
  • A tool for reflection and clarity
  • A way to notice energy & context

What the gears are not

  • Not labels or permanent
  • Not a hierarchy
  • Not a measure of success
Answer honestly, not aspirationally. Choose what feels true, not impressive. If you feel unsure between gears — that uncertainty is information. Take one breath before you begin. This is about alignment, not optimisation.
